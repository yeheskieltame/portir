title: "Switch between testnet and mainnet"
description: "Try everything with free test money first."
---

  <img src="/images/d-profile.jpg" alt="Testnet or mainnet, one switch" />
</Frame>

Go to **Profile → Mode**.

- **Testnet** (default): buys settle on BSC testnet with tUSDT against test stock tokens, at the live mainnet price. Tap **Get 10,000 tUSDT** on Profile; no cooldown.
- **Mainnet**: real USDT and real stock tokens on BNB Chain.

See [Testnet and mainnet](/learn/testnet-and-mainnet) for what runs where.
