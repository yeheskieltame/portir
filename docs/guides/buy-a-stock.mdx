<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the stock">
    Markets lists 500+ US stocks and ETFs. Search by name or ticker, or filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Guard">
    The stock page shows the session, the on-chain vs exchange price, the issuers compared, and the verdict with its sentence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Tap **Buy** and enter USDT. You see the expected shares and the guaranteed minimum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Your wallet signs an approval (once per token) and the swap. If the verdict is **BLOCK**, you are offered **Buy when it's fair** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>
