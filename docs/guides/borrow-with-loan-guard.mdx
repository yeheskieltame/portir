<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Loans">Pick a stock Venus accepts as collateral: NVIDIA, Tesla, SpaceX or SK Hynix.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a borrow size">The slider sets the borrow as a share of your borrow limit (10% to 90%).</Step>
  <Step title="Protect">Keep **Protect** on. Set the cap per rescue (default 200 USDT) and the safety buffer.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign the batch">One batch: buy the stock through the Guard → supply as collateral → borrow USDT → set the guard → approve the buffer. On testnet it includes 10,000 free tUSDT if you need it.</Step>
</Steps>

The Loans page then shows your used limit, the guard's trigger and target, and every rescue the agent made. Cancel the guard or revoke the buffer at any time.
