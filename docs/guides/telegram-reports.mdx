<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile → Telegram alerts">Connect your wallet first.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Telegram button">It opens the Portir bot with your wallet address as the start parameter.</Step>
  <Step title="Press Start">The bot links that address to your chat.</Step>
</Steps>

From then on you receive one message per recorded outcome (✅ Executed, ⏸ Waited, ⏭ Skipped) with the reason and a BscScan link, plus every Loan Guard rescue. The bot is read-only: it cannot move funds.
