---
title: "Connect a wallet"
description: "Use any browser wallet on BNB Chain. No account, no sign-up."
---

<Frame caption="Connect wallet, top right">
  <img src="/images/d-markets.jpg" alt="Connect wallet, top right" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a wallet">
    Any injected wallet works: **Binance Wallet**, **MetaMask** and others. On a phone, open `app.portir.xyz` inside the wallet's own in-app browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Tap **Connect** in the top right and approve the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep a little BNB">
    Every transaction pays gas in BNB. About $0.05 of BNB covers a purchase. On testnet use the [BNB Chain faucet](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/testnet-faucet).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The app asks your wallet to switch network when needed (testnet or mainnet, depending on the mode).
</Note>
