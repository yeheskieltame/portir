---
title: "Portfolio"
description: "Live balances, history and dividends."
---

The Portfolio page reads your stock-token balances from the chain and shows them as **shares** (balance × issuer multiplier) at today's on-chain price, with charts, purchase history and dividend information.

</Frame>

<Note>
Cost basis comes from purchases made in the app on this device (stored locally in your browser) until Portir has an indexer. Holdings themselves are always read from the chain.
</Note>
