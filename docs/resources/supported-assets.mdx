| BSC testnet | 97 | Plans, sell rules, Loan Guard, test buys |

## Stocks and ETFs

The catalog lists **500+ US stocks and ETFs** on BNB Chain, read live from the Binance RWA Data API, across three issuers:

| Issuer | Token suffix | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ondo | `on` | `NVDAon` |
| bStocks | `B` | `NVDAB` |
| xStocks | `x` | `NVDAx` |

On testnet, the 20 featured and basket tickers have test tokens: AAPL, AMD, AMZN, AVGO, GOOGL, IWM, JNJ, KO, MCD, META, MSFT, NVDA, PEP, PG, QQQ, SPY, TSLA, TSM, SPCX, SKHY.

## Baskets

AI & Semis, Big Tech, Dividend Blue-chips, US Broad Market. See [Baskets](/learn/baskets).

## Payment token

**USDT** on BNB Chain (18 decimals); **tUSDT** on testnet. Gas is paid in **BNB**.

## Loan collateral

NVIDIA, Tesla, SpaceX and SK Hynix (bStocks tokens accepted by Venus). See [Loan Guard](/learn/loan-guard).

## Wallets

Any injected browser wallet (Binance Wallet, MetaMask, …). The agent trades on mainnet through a **Binance Agentic Wallet**.
