A basket is a fixed list of holdings and weights. Buying a basket (by hand or in a plan) runs one guarded swap per holding, in sequence. There is no basket token: every share lands in your wallet as its own stock token.

## How the Guard judges a basket

A basket is judged by its **worst holding**: if one holding is BLOCK, the whole basket waits. Smart timing requires every holding's market to be open.

## Partial fills

If one holding fails to buy, the others are not retried (that would double-buy them). The run is recorded as Executed with "Bought 5/6 holdings. Failed: …", and the unspent USDT is returned.
