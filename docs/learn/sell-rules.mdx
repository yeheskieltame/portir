---
title: "Sell rules"
description: "Take-profit and stop-loss orders the agent executes when the real price crosses your trigger."
---

A sell rule is a one-time plan that sells shares you already hold.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Stock token | The token to sell |
| Shares | Exactly how many shares to sell |
| Trigger price | Per-share price in USD |
| Direction | **Take-profit** (sell at or above) or **stop-loss** (sell at or below) |

## How it runs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Watch">The agent reads the stock's real exchange price every 15 minutes. No time limit.</Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the open">Once the trigger is crossed, it waits until the US market is open.</Step>
  <Step title="Pull exactly the rule's shares">`pullShares` moves the shares from your wallet through `PlanRegistry`. You approve the registry for that token.</Step>
  <Step title="Sell and deliver">It sells at a fair on-chain price and sends the USDT to your wallet. If the sale fails, the shares are returned and the run is logged as Waited.</Step>
</Steps>
