<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app and connect">
    Go to [app.portir.xyz](https://app.portir.xyz) and connect a browser wallet (Binance Wallet or MetaMask; on a phone, open the app inside the wallet's own browser). See [Connect a wallet](/guides/connect-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay on testnet">
    The app starts in **testnet** mode (Profile → Mode). Get a little tBNB for gas from the [BNB Chain faucet](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/testnet-faucet), then tap **Get 10,000 tUSDT** on the Profile page (no cooldown).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a stock and read the Guard">
    Open NVIDIA (testnet supports the 20 featured stocks and every basket holding). Under the chart you see the session, the on-chain price against the exchange price, and a verdict: **GO**, **WARN** or **BLOCK** with one sentence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Tap **Buy**, enter an amount and sign. On testnet the trade settles on `TestExchange` at a quote signed with the live mainnet price. The shares appear under Portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a plan">
    Plans → New. Choose a stock or a basket, an amount per run and a cadence (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly). Turn on **Smart timing** to wait for the US open. Sign the plan, then tap **Allow** so the registry may fund it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the agent">
    Every 15 minutes the agent checks your plan. Each run shows up on the plan page and its flow diagram as Executed, Waited or Skipped, with the reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Link Telegram from Profile to get a message for every run. See [Telegram reports](/guides/telegram-reports).
</Tip>
